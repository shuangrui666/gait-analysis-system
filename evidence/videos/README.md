# Original experiment video evidence

This directory documents the 29 original MP4 recordings recovered from the submitted project-stage archive. The videos are preserved in the original archive and are indexed by filename, file size, and SHA-256 in `video_manifest.csv`.

These recordings provide supporting evidence that the acquisition sessions were actually carried out: the footage shows the experiment activities and phone placement/operation. They should be evaluated together with the corresponding Phyphox CSV exports and metadata. The archive-entry timestamps are packaging timestamps, not capture timestamps.

Important: this manifest is an integrity and evidence index, not a claim that every video is uniquely paired with one sensor ZIP. No video-to-ZIP one-to-one mapping is asserted.
